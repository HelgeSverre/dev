Under the command

Four steps. No magic.

  1. Scan locally

    Read a bounded index of manifests, configs, targets, and workspace structure. Respect ignores.

  2. Build candidates

    Detectors turn project evidence into explicit programs, argv, working directories, and context.

  3. Rank the evidence

    Intent, proximity, availability, and your hints produce one deterministic ordering.

  4. Execute exactly

    A clear winner inherits your terminal. Ambiguity opens the picker. No shell wrapper is added.

Built to explain itself

Ask why before anything runs.

Add --why to inspect candidates, evidence, hint matches, and the final decision. Use --dry-run when you only want the resolved shell command.
